### Week 13

[Refactoring the RPG Engine – Map Actions, and Debug Pain [Dev Log #13]](https://youtu.be/9_20Al4WblE)

This dev log covers a big ol' refactor to my custom RPG Engine for Space Engineers. After a recent update broke how the engine loads, I decided to shift how actions are stored—moving map-specific actions into the map data instead of stuffing everything into global GameData. It seemed simple. But of course... nothing's ever that easy.

Wurst AI hosts as I (the dev) slowly unravel a series of hilarious and frustrating bugs—mostly caused by subsystems still using the old logic. Menus? Broken. Shops? Very broken. NPCs? Gaslighting me. But it all works now. Probably.

As always, I’m communicating via AAC—so shout out to Juniper for editing around my slow chaotic typing. Rubber duck debugging and glazed chaos ahead.
