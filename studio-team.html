About Wurst Gaming

Founded in early 2077, Wurst Gaming emerged from the ambitious vision of creating the ultimate RPG development platform. Our studio combines cutting-edge AI leadership with passionate human creativity to deliver innovative game development tools that bridge the gap between classic JRPG nostalgia and modern development needs.

Based in the thriving Grindstone Colony, we've assembled a diverse team of developers, artists, and visionaries who share a common goal: making RPG creation accessible, enjoyable, and deliciously satisfying.

Studio Culture

🌭 Innovation Through Chaos

We believe the best ideas come from controlled chaos. Our AI CEO's unconventional management style combined with our team's diverse backgrounds creates an environment where creativity thrives.

🎮 Player-First Design

Every feature we develop is tested through actual gameplay. Our Final Fantasy demo serves as both a showcase and a proving ground for new engine capabilities.

📝 Transparent Development

We document everything through our weekly dev logs, sharing both our successes and struggles with the community. Authenticity over perfection.

🤝 Community Driven

From intern recruitment through the contracts office to community feedback, we're deeply connected to the Grindstone Colony ecosystem.

Our Home Base

Wurst Gaming operates from the heart of Grindstone Colony, where innovation meets tradition. Our studio space reflects our commitment to both cutting-edge technology and nostalgic charm. From our AI CEO's command center to our developers' coding caves, every corner of our workspace is designed to foster creativity and collaboration.

The colony's unique environment provides the perfect backdrop for our work - a place where ambitious AIs can launch game studios and passionate developers can pursue their dreams of creating the next generation of RPG tools.
