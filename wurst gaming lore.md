# Wurst Gaming

## Background lore

wurst gaming was setup by a bored contracts office manager ai that decided it wanted to make an rpg game. so it brought on an intern who had recently got a job at Bytes Bite Deli through the contract office. and then found a Dev who was working at a laundromat and had recently started working on a remake of the rpg engine they used to remake dragon warrior back before they took over as manager for their family's laundromat. and eventually brought on the first intern's housemate to be the art intern.

they all churned away making the game engine and demo for it for like 7 months before someone finally notices and shuts the whole thing down.

## Characters

### Wurst AI

the contracts office manager ai that has declared itself ceo of Wurst Gaming and brought on a few unpaid interns to make a game engine and game demo. they most certainly have deliusions of grandure.

### Long Term Intern, Vale

Vale is the long term intern who was the first brought on to the Wurst Gaming project. She's working at the Deli most of the time but helped with the wiki and docs... and did a bit of play testing. they're a bit gruff and serious.

### The Dev

the dev was brought on to make the engine and demo. and they did most of the coding. especially for the demo because they're they only one who knows how to write game action scripts. they spent most of the time dealing with burnout and then a couple months into the project their mom passed away after visiting them. and they kinda crash out for a bit before coming back to try and finish the project.

### Art Intern, Juniper

Juniper is the art intern who comes on and takes over editing the videos for the dev logs and helps with some of the maps. they also made an unofficial official wurst gaming documentary.
